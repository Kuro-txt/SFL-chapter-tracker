📖 CHAPTER TRACKER GUIDE

💾 1. SAVE BEFORE CLOSING
• Always click ☁️ SAVE IN CLOUD before you close your browser tab or shut down your PC. This writes your latest daily delivery snapshot and weekly tasks into your permanent cloud vault.
🥞 2. AUTOMATED STACKED DELIVERIES
• The tracker checks NPC delivery milestone jumps automatically. If you complete yesterday's saved delivery and today's new delivery from the same NPC on the same day, the stacked order is automatically injected into today's list with full ticket yield.
⚡ 3. 2x DOUBLE DELIVERY EVENTS
• When a 2x Delivery event is active in the game calendar, the tracker automatically multiplies your total ticket yield: (Base + VIP + Boosts) × 2 for your first completed delivery of the day.
🛤️ 4. CHAPTER TRACK & MANUAL ADJUSTMENTS
• Use the 🛤️ TRACK section on the dashboard to log tickets and SFL spent on the chapter reward track, or to adjust for any missed tickets, price mismatches, or special event items.
🎁 5. DAILY LOGIN REWARD (+1 TICKET)
• Your daily login ticket auto-increments by +1 once every calendar day when you visit the site or via the automated 23:00 UTC server cron. You can also manually adjust the count directly in the number box.
👑 6. BOOSTS & VIP BONUSES
• VIP (+2): Adds +2 bonus tickets to all NPC Deliveries and Chores.
• +1 Boosts (#1-#3): Adds +1 ticket per active booster across all deliveries, bounties, and chores.
