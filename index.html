📖 CHAPTER TRACKER GUIDE

🚀 1. DAILY 3-STEP ROUTINE
• ① 📦 COMPLETE TASKS: Finish your orders, chores, or bounties in-game.
• ② 🌾 FETCH DATA: Syncs your completed tasks & live prices — it automatically saves your progress to your Cloud Vault!
• ③ ☁️ SAVE IN CLOUD: When manually adding tickets into the Track or adjusting goals, click SAVE IN CLOUD to lock them in.
💡 Note: Sync when you are done with deliveries, chores, and bounties for the day to ensure your ticket count is accurate. We have an automatic sync that runs at 23:00 UTC too but don't trust it too much.
📈 2. CLICK BARS FOR WEEKLY BREAKDOWNS
• Hover or Tap: Hover or tap any week bar in the Weekly Tickets Progression chart to see a breakdown popup of tickets earned from Deliveries, Bounties, Animal Bounties, and Chores.
• Click Bar: Opens the full itemized list of orders and tasks completed during that specific week with SFL costs.
⚡ 3. 2x DOUBLE DELIVERY EVENTS
• First Delivery 2x: On 2x event days, your first completed delivery for each NPC gives double tickets: [(Base + VIP + Boosts) × 2].
• Extra Orders: Any additional orders completed for the same NPC on that day give standard 1x tickets.
👑 4. VIP, BOOSTERS & DAILY LOGIN
• VIP (+2): Toggle the VIP checkbox at the top to automatically add +2 tickets to all Deliveries & Chores.
• Boosters (+1): Toggle your active boosters to add +1 ticket each.
• Daily Login (+1): +1 ticket is automatically credited every calendar day.
✏️ 5. EDITING & CUSTOM TASKS
• 📜 EDIT: Click 📜 EDIT on any card to view your history, filter by week, or filter by NPC.
• Active-First: Pending tasks always stay at the top. Completed orders are sorted by date.
• Custom Items: You can add or adjust tickets/costs for any past or current week at any time.
