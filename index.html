📖 CHAPTER TRACKER GUIDE

💾 1. SAVE IN CLOUD VAULT
• Click ☁️ SAVE IN CLOUD anytime you make changes to securely sync your completed deliveries, chores, bounties, and tickets to your database vault.
📅 2. WEEK-BY-WEEK LOGGING & EDITING
• Click 📜 EDIT on any category card to view full item history, filter by specific weeks (Week 1 through Week 12), or manually add custom deliveries, chores, or bounties for any week.
⚡ 3. 2x DOUBLE DELIVERY EVENTS
• When a Double Delivery event is active, the first completed delivery of each NPC today receives 2x total tickets: [(Base + VIP + Boosts) × 2].
🎁 4. DAILY LOGIN & TRACK ADJUSTMENTS
• 🎁 LOGIN: Automatically records +1 ticket per calendar day into your total ticket counter.
• 🛤️ TRACK: Use the Track inputs to log chapter reward milestones, adjustments, or manual SFL costs.
👑 5. VIP & BOOST MULTIPLIERS
• VIP (+2): Adds +2 bonus tickets to NPC Deliveries and Chores.
• +1 Boosts (#1–#3): Adds +1 ticket per active booster across your activities.
